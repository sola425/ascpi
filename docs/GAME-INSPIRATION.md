The table describes the agreed Labwise MVP direction, which is being implemented, and proposed refinements. It is not a claim that these patterns improve exam results.

## Highest-value next refinements

1. **Make results useful:** show first-attempt correct, corrected after review, and needs revisit separately. A recovery answer is progress without erasing the original error.
2. **Connect today's game to later recall:** offer “Review due items” as a daily quest; save forgotten memory checks to the existing review queue. Explain any due date in plain language.
3. **Make rewards predictable:** award XP once under a visible, capped rule. Replaying the same item cannot produce unlimited XP. Quests reward a completed study action, not opening a screen or staying online.
4. **Keep return easy:** retain earned cosmetics after missed days. Avoid lost streaks, lives, random reward boxes and content locks. Cosmetic lab upgrades do not imply subject mastery, exam readiness or professional competence.
5. **Keep voice under learner control:** show the recognized choice or transcript for review and require Submit. Read-aloud, speech capture and the optional answer clock stop or pause before explanations. Never auto-submit uncertain speech or count recognition failure as an incorrect answer.

These are design ideas only. No external code, artwork, logos, audio, exercises or question content were copied. No biological content was reviewed for this note. Source review establishes what the projects document; learning benefit requires observing later recall and review behavior in Labwise.
